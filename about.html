About Us

Zach

Growing up, I witnessed firsthand the struggles of foreclosure, an experience that left a lasting impact. The turmoil of that time shaped my understanding of hardship and the importance of stability. As I grew older and worked as an electrician, I discovered a spark for real estate, seeing it as an opportunity to do something I love—helping people in challenging situations. Real estate became more than just a career; it became a way for me to transform difficult circumstances into something beautiful, while creating positive change for others. Now, I’m dedicated to using my skills and passion to help others turn their dreams into reality, just as I’m working to do for myself.

Jordan

Jordan got his start in construction at a young age, helping his parents with repairs and remodeling projects around his childhood home. That early hands-on experience sparked a lifelong interest in building and design. With over two decades in the construction industry, Jordan is passionate about transforming spaces, leaving things better than he found them, and delivering exceptional service. His work is driven by a deep appreciation for both construction and real estate—two industries that continue to fuel his creativity and purpose.

New Path

Founded in 2024, our company came to life after a chance meeting at a local real estate meetup. With backgrounds in real estate, renovation, and problem-solving, we realized our skills were the perfect match. From that moment on, we teamed up with a shared mission: to breathe new life into distressed properties and revitalize neighborhoods one home at a time.

We specialize in purchasing and transforming distressed properties—turning challenges into opportunities, both for us and the communities we serve. Whether it's a fixer-upper or a property in need of a little love, we're passionate about creating value where others might see obstacles.

And yes, that’s an alpaca you see in our logo! Our mascot represents our approach: friendly, hardworking, and just a little different. Like the alpaca, we’re built for tough terrain—but we always do it with style and a smile.

Top 6 Reasons

Why Choose Us?

With so many home buying business out there, why choose us?

Transparency & Honesty

When it comes time to sell your house for a cash offer, we want you to choose us. And we think the best way to make that happen is to be transparent and fair from the start. That's why we'll always be open about what our cash offer is and how we arrived at it.

Reputation

Like any small business we live and die by our reviews. Throughout the whole process our aim is to earn a five star review from you just like we have dozens of others.

Speed & Flexibility

We understand that timing can be just as important a factor as money when choosing to sell your home for cash. We're dedicated to ensuring we can close every deal on your timeline.

No Hidden Fees or Costs

Did we mention that our cash offers are upfront, transparent and fair? As part of that same commitment to transparency we will never include hidden or last minute fees or costs. The offer is the offer, full stop from day one.

Professionalism & Communication

Even when choosing the easiest path of selling your home for cash, selling a home is still a big move. That's why we're committed to always being responsive and available to field your questions.

The Offer Itself

While we pride ourselves on being professional and easy to work with, we know that at the end of the day it's all about the money. That's why our first offer will always be a fair one. We'll never low-ball you.
